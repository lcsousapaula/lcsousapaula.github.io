Phlebotomine sand flies are diverse vectors whose evolutionary relationships and biology remain important areas for research. I combine systematics with genomic and microbiome approaches to study diversity, evolutionary history, and vector–pathogen relationships, with an emphasis on research questions relevant to Brazil.

I am interested in collaborations that connect field collections, specimen identification, and molecular or computational analyses.
