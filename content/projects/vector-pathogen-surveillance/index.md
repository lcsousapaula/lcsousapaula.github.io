Understanding which pathogens circulate in vector populations depends on robust molecular methods and careful interpretation of ecological and taxonomic context. My work and collaborations examine vector–pathogen associations and explore molecular approaches that can contribute to research and surveillance of vector-borne diseases.

I welcome discussions with groups working on pathogen detection, assay development, field surveillance, and genomic data analysis.
