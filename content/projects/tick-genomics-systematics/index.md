Reliable genomic resources can strengthen tick identification, comparative biology, and evolutionary research. This work brings together reference genome development, molecular systematics, and phylogenomic analyses to investigate relationships within medically and veterinary important tick groups.

I welcome collaborations involving tick specimens, genomic data, taxonomy, and bioinformatics.
